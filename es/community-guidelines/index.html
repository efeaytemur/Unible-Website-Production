Building the Unible Community

It starts with an interactive student ecosystem, designed to bridge the gap between academic collaboration and vibrant campus social life. Whether you are searching for a startup co-founder to launch a new venture, looking for a peer to crush your upcoming exams, or simply wanting to share a genuine fika break, Unible opens the door to endless possibilities. We are building the infrastructure for professional growth and authentic real-world connections, and every verified university student is welcome to explore.

We want Unible to be a safe, inclusive, kind place where anyone can seamlessly network while being their authentic selves. These Community Guidelines set clear expectations for everyone’s behavior, both on and off the app. So please read on. Not following these guidelines can have real consequences, ranging from an immediate account ban to legal action.

Unible's Rules:

1. Respect Boundaries. Comfort levels vary, but remember that Unible is a campus network. Unible is a space for meaningful campus connections, whether for a study session, a project collaboration, or a fika break. Unible is a dual space designed for both your academic career and your campus social life. All public profiles must remain campus appropriate. We do not allow nudity, explicit sexual content, or graphic material on public cards. While private chats are a space for authentic conversation, our core safety rules apply everywhere on Unible. Non-consensual sexual content, exploitation, harassment, toxic behavior, or unwanted persistence are strictly prohibited in both public profiles and private messages, and will result in an immediate and permanent ban.

2. Share cautiously and thoughtfully. To protect your privacy and ensure safety on campus, do not publicly broadcast your personal contact details on your profile cards (such as phone numbers, private emails, or social media handles). Let the connection happen securely within Unible first. Sharing your bank account numbers or email password is always a bad idea. Don’t ask others to send you their personal details either. Be cautious: if someone asks you to send them money or tries to get you to make an investment, it’s probably a scam.

3. Steer clear of violent content. We promote campus positivity, safety, and constructive networking. We have zero tolerance for violent content involving gore, death, hateful imagery, or other harmful material. This includes graphic images, descriptions of violent acts (against humans or animals), use of weapons, hate speech targeting student groups, or anything that advocates self-harm. If our system or safety team detects a risk of imminent student safety violations, we will take immediate steps to restrict the account and provide crisis support resources.

4. Make personal connections, not commercial ones. Unible is built for real peer-to-peer networking, study partnerships, dating, and campus socializing—not commercial exploitation. While we strongly encourage promoting student union/Nation events, searching for startup co-founders, or organizing study groups, we strictly prohibit commercial spam, selling products, or aggressive self-promotion. Furthermore, Unible is a space for genuine human connections; any form of compensated relationships, escort services, or non-academic commercial solicitation is strictly forbidden.

5. Visual & Activity Integrity (Photos & Campus Challenges). Unible is a visual and active campus space. Whether you are uploading photos to your profile, sharing your daily campus updates, or creating Campus Activities, your content drives the community culture. To keep Unible authentic and safe, you must follow these visual guidelines:

Keep it Authentic and Present. Upload photos that represent the real you, your student life, hobbies, or academic projects. We strictly prohibit using internet stock photos, heavily altered AI replicas, or impersonating another student.

Respect Public Spaces and Consent. When posting photos of your campus activities, study groups, or Fika sessions, ensure you have the consent of the people in the photo. Do not take or upload non-consensual photos of other students in libraries, classrooms, or student union buildings.

No Inappropriate or Explicit Imagery. While beach photos or casual campus outfits are perfectly fine, we do not allow explicit nudity, suggestive underwear shots, or sexually graphic imagery on public profile cards or challenge feeds. Furthermore, sharing non-consensual sexual content or intimate images without permission is strictly banned anywhere on Unible, including in private chats.

Safe and Constructive Activities. When creating a Campus Activity (e.g., study marathons, campus sports, or group gatherings), the action must be safe and lawful. We strictly ban any challenges that involve hazing, illegal drinking games, trespassing, or risking physical harm to yourself or other students.

6. Communicate Respectfully. Talking to new people can be tricky, especially when you’re interacting with humans from all walks of life. Respect goes a long way.

If the conversation goes unexpectedly awry and you find yourself getting upset or feeling angry—pause and reflect before you react. Harassment, threats, bullying, intimidation, doxing, sextortion, blackmail, or anything intentionally done to cause harm is not allowed.

Unible is not a place for hate. We will never stand behind racism, bigotry, hatred, or violence based on who someone is, how they identify, or what they look like. This includes, but is not limited to, someone’s race, ethnicity, nationality, immigration status, caste, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, body type, or health status. If you see someone who doesn't meet your personal criteria, don't contact them and move on. Don't report them unless you think they've violated our policies.

7. Lead with kindness, not harm. Any acts or behavior that suggests, intends, or causes harm to another user—either on or offline, physically or digitally—will be taken very seriously. This includes anything calling for or inciting harm.

If you have been hurt by someone on Unible: first, please take care of yourself, and second, take some time to decide what you need to heal, whether that be accountability measures, disclosure, support, or all of the above. If this includes reporting the harm to us, please reach out. We are here for you.

8. Verified university community only. Unible is an exclusive, trusted ecosystem built for verified university communities—including current students, alumni, and university staff. To maintain a secure space for both your academic networking and your social life, we enforce a strict age and campus verification policy. You must be 18 years of age or older and hold a valid, verifiable email address issued by an accredited higher education institution (.se, .no, .dk, .edu, or equivalent) to use Unible. This is why verifying your university email address (.edu.se or equivalent) is mandatory during signup.

No Minors or Childhood Photos. Because Unible is also a space for potential real-world meetings and campus socializing, we do not allow photos of unaccompanied or unclothed minors. This includes uploading photos of your younger self, no matter how adorable or retro you looked in middle school. Every photo on your profile card must represent your current self.

Alumni and Faculty Discretion. If you have recently graduated or are part of the university staff, you may use Unible for project development or professional coffee breaks, provided you respect the peer-to-peer nature of the student community.

9. Abide by the law. No illegal content or activities are allowed, ever. This means you can’t use Unible to buy or sell drugs or counterfeit goods, or ask for assistance to help you break the law. We definitely won’t tolerate anyone using Unible to advocate or participate in any sort of harm involving minors or human trafficking.

10. One person, one account. Each account can only have one owner. For logistical and privacy reasons, we can’t support multiple people accessing the same account; each individual needs to have their own. Also, one person can’t have more than one account at a time.

11. This is your space, post your own content. Don’t post images or private messages from other people unless you’ve been given consent to do so. Don’t post work that’s copyrighted or trademarked by others.

12. Be a good Unible user. Don’t abuse Unible. Don’t use Unible to spread false or misleading information. Don’t spam harmful links or unsolicited content. Don’t create mass accounts. Don’t use Unible to manipulate, con, or get people to send you money or anything else. It's okay to talk to your friends and family about people you are meeting, but don't publicly share someone else's information. Don’t submit false, misleading, or malicious reports. Don’t use third-party apps or other technology to unlock features or game the system. Don’t harass, threaten, or otherwise violate these rules when speaking directly with Unible or its affiliates.

13. Stick around to stay active. If you don’t log into your Unible account in two years, we’ll assume it’s not being used and may delete it for inactivity. So if you want to be seen in the app, just log in from time to time.

Reporting

As a member of the Unible community, we encourage you to speak up and speak out. You can use the in-app Report button directly on any user profile, plan, or message to flag a concern, or tap Block on a profile to immediately prevent that user from contacting you. You can also email us anytime at helpunible@gmail.com. We review all reports promptly within 24 hours of receipt. Your report is kept confidential, except where disclosure is required by law or strictly necessary to investigate and handle the report fairly. If you believe a moderation or reporting decision was made in error, you may appeal it free of charge within six months by emailing helpunible@gmail.com, as detailed in our Terms of Use.

Impact

We take our Community Guidelines and the impact they have on our community seriously. We’ll do everything we can to make sure people follow them. We have a warning system in place, but if violations continue or if the violation is severe, we will respond accordingly.

We reserve the right to investigate and/or terminate accounts without a refund of any purchases if we find you have misused the Service or behaved in a way Unible deems inappropriate, unlawful, or in violation of our Community Guidelines or Terms of Use, including actions or communications that occur off the Service but involve others you meet through the Service.
