Privacy policy

Your privacy matters. We only use your information to operate and improve your experience on Unible.

You can review your profile information, update your profile, and request access to or deletion of your data. We do not sell or share personal information.

Read privacy policy

Protect the vibe! Community guidelines

Real connections require real respect. Impersonation, harassment, hate speech, threats, and non-consensual content have no place on campus. Use accurate profile information, respect other people’s boundaries, and keep every conversation considerate.

Read community guidelines

Smart meetups

Take a moment to chat on Unible before taking things offline, and never share passwords or financial details.

When you meet up—whether for a 15-minute fika or a study session—stick to public campus spots or busy cafés, let a friend know where you’re headed, and arrange your own transportation.

Reporting and blocking

If someone crosses a line or makes you uncomfortable, block and report them in seconds. Every report stays strictly confidential and is reviewed directly by our team—fast, fair, and without drama.

Include any relevant details when you report an issue so the team can respond quickly and fairly.

Get help

For account or safety questions, contact our support team. Reach out to our team anytime at support@unible.app — we’re always happy to help. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first.

Contact support

Account and data deletion

We’ll miss having you on campus! Whenever you're ready to move on, you can permanently delete your account and all your data right from your profile settings in the app.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can easily request deletion using our website below.
