Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Unible collects, why we process it, how it is protected, and the rights available to you.

1. Operator and scope

Unible (unible.app) is operated by Efe Aytemur, an independent software developer based in Beşiktaş, İstanbul, Türkiye, who acts as the data controller under the GDPR. In this Privacy Policy, “Unible”, “we”, and “us” refer to the Unible service and its operator. For any privacy questions or data requests, you can reach us at helpunible@gmail.com.

This Policy covers Unible Network, Unible Lounge and support. Our Terms of Use and Community Guidelines are available on this site. This Privacy Policy is also published here. Accepting these documents does not give consent to optional processing.

Unible serves verified university students, alumni and staff aged 18 or over, primarily in the EU/EEA. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Contact us if you believe a minor has an account; we will investigate and delete their data, except any minimal information lawfully needed for safeguarding or legal obligations.

2. Personal data we collect

• Account and verification: name, age or date of birth, university email address, university, faculty or major, and graduation or affiliation status. Email endings such as .se, .no, .dk and .edu do not alone prove eligibility.

• Profile and content: profile photos, bio, interests, campus plans and activities, and in-app chat messages.

• Safety: reports, supporting evidence, blocked-account lists, moderation outcomes and automated text/image safety logs.

• Technical: authentication tokens, device type, operating-system version, IP addresses, crash logs and installation identifiers used by authentication and crash-reporting services. These serve security, authentication and app stability, not advertising.

• Optional purchase status: if you purchase optional Paid Features, boosts or subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play, we receive purchase-confirmation, entitlement and subscription-status information to verify and manage access. We never collect or store credit-card or banking details.

Information comes from you, your use of the app, other users’ messages or reports, and the service providers described below. Support emails contain the information you send us. Required fields are identified during registration; without essential age, account and university verification information, we cannot provide an account. Optional details are not required to use core features.

3. How and why we process data

• Contract — Article 6(1)(b): create and authenticate accounts, verify university eligibility, display core profiles and plans, deliver messages and provide requested features, including verifying or restoring access to Paid Features.

• Legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f): keep Unible secure, prevent spam, abuse and ban evasion, moderate content, review reports within 24 hours, provide support and fix bugs. We assess necessity and balance these interests against your rights.

• Legal obligations — Article 6(1)(c): meet applicable EU/EEA legal duties, including required handling of DSA illegal-content notices and legally binding requests. We assess requests from other countries against applicable data-protection and disclosure rules.

• Consent — Article 6(1)(a): process optional profile details or send optional communications where you opt in. Withdraw consent through the relevant app setting or by emailing us; earlier lawful processing remains unaffected. Where device-access rules require consent for diagnostic collection, we obtain it before collection.

Avoid unnecessary sensitive details. We obtain separate, explicit consent before optional features collect health, religion, sexual orientation or similar special-category data. Simply posting information is not explicit consent. Sensitive report evidence is processed only under an applicable Article 9 condition, such as necessity for legal claims, explained when relevant data is collected.

Unible uses no third-party advertising networks or behavioural tracking pixels and never sells personal data.

4. Sharing and service providers

Other users see the profile details and plans you share through the relevant app features. Messages are shared with their intended participants. Providers process messages for delivery and safety; the developer may access relevant content only when necessary for support, technical investigation or moderation. Other users may independently save content they receive.

We use Google Firebase and related Google Cloud services for authentication, databases, cloud storage, crash reporting and automated text/image safety filtering. These providers process necessary data on our behalf under applicable data-processing terms and may use authorised subprocessors. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail and may be processed outside the EEA under Google’s applicable terms.

If you purchase Paid Features, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the purchase and provides verification information. Apple and Google are independent controllers for their own app-store services and transaction records under their privacy notices.

We disclose information to competent authorities or legal advisers only where lawfully required or necessary to protect rights. Reports are handled with restricted access; we do not promise absolute confidentiality where disclosure is legally required or strictly necessary to handle a case fairly.

5. International data transfers

Our primary application database and media storage are configured in Google’s europe-west3 region in Frankfurt, Germany. Firebase locations are set per service; Frankfurt hosting does not mean that every service processes data only in the EU. Firebase Authentication operates in the United States. Crashlytics and some supporting Google services, including safety processing, may process data globally.

Unible is operated from Türkiye, and the developer may securely access personal data remotely only as necessary for support, bug fixing and safety or moderation reviews. We use encrypted connections, access controls and limited administrative access. Türkiye does not have an EU adequacy decision; encryption alone does not replace a required transfer safeguard.

For covered transfers to Google in the US, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework while its adequacy decision and Google’s relevant certification remain valid. Other restricted transfers use applicable European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, including relevant provider-to-controller transfers to Türkiye, with transfer assessments and additional safeguards where necessary. You may request information or a copy of applicable safeguards by email, with confidential details redacted where appropriate.

6. Safety, moderation and automated processing

Automated text/image checks identify suspected prohibited material and may filter content for review. We use reports and relevant moderation records to enforce the Terms of Use and Community Guidelines. A human decides account suspensions or permanent bans and reviews challenges; we do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects solely by automated means.

Use Report or Block in the app for safety concerns. Reports receive review within 24 hours, although complex investigations may take longer. You may challenge moderation decisions free of charge by emailing us within six months, as explained in the Terms of Use.

7. Data retention

Account data, profiles and content are kept while an account is active unless deleted earlier. Inactive accounts may be deleted after extended periods of inactivity following advance email notice. Purchase-status records are kept only while needed to verify access or resolve related disputes. Security, support and moderation records are kept only to resolve incidents, handle related challenges or meet legal preservation duties.

8. Account deletion

You can request permanent deletion at any time through:

• The in-app Delete Account button.

• The account-deletion option at unible.app.

• helpunible@gmail.com.

We delete your account, associated personal data and content from Unible’s active application systems without undue delay and promptly instruct providers to delete associated data. Uninstalling alone does not delete the account. Account deletion does not itself cancel an app-store subscription; manage cancellation in your app-store settings.

Deleted data in encrypted application backups controlled by Unible is cleared within a 30-day cycle. Backups are isolated from ordinary operations; deletion is reapplied after restoration. Google’s underlying provider systems may take up to 180 days to complete deletion, including Firebase Authentication’s live and backup systems. Crashlytics keeps crash records and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal. Authentication IP logs are generally kept for a few weeks. The 30-day cycle does not cover every Firebase service.

Limited retention may continue where strictly required by law. To prevent a banned user from re-registering, we may retain only a minimal account identifier and ban record where proportionate and necessary for that legitimate interest. We review its necessity at least every six months and delete it when no longer needed. Retention never authorises continued public display of deleted content. We cannot erase copies independently saved by other users outside our control.

9. Your GDPR rights

Subject to applicable GDPR conditions, you may:

• Access your personal data and obtain a copy.

• Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.

• Erase data, including through account deletion.

• Restrict processing in qualifying circumstances.

• Receive or transfer data you provided, in a machine-readable format, where processing is automated and based on consent or contract.

• Object to processing based on legitimate interests. We stop unless we demonstrate compelling overriding grounds or need the data for legal claims.

• Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior lawful processing.

Email helpunible@gmail.com. We may request proportionate identity verification and normally respond free of charge within one month. If a lawful extension is needed, we explain why within that month; the extension may be up to two further months.

10. Complaints

You may complain to the authority where you live or work, or where the alleged infringement occurred: for example, IMY in Sweden (https://www.imy.se), Datatilsynet in Norway (https://www.datatilsynet.no), or Datatilsynet in Denmark (https://www.datatilsynet.dk). You do not need our permission or have to contact us first to exercise that right.

11. Changes and contact

We explain material changes through the app or email and update the date below. We obtain fresh consent where required for new optional purposes. Contact us at helpunible@gmail.com for any privacy questions or requests.
